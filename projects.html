01 AI learning tool · USCA coaching simulator for new managers 1 decision, 3 levels of impact +

Problem

First-time managers can't see how one decision lands on the person, the team, and the business at the same time.

Approach

An AI practice space with realistic personas, branching scenarios, and consequences shown at all three levels.

Result

A working prototype, with a roadmap toward a fuller leadership practice ground.

02 Learning in the productHelp at the moment of need 3x feature use +

Problem

Customers missed useful features and opened support cases they didn't need to.

Approach

An in-product messaging program: guidance where people work, a resource center, and a "hidden gems" spotlight on the product homepage.

Result

One key feature tripled in use, with 20% fewer support cases than expected. In-app invites drove 2–3x more event sign-ups.

03 TrainingRepeatable onboarding for a writing team 15+ new hires +

Problem

New technical writers had a lot of tools and workflows to learn at once, and training lived in different places.

Approach

I built one onboarding path with a single source of truth for training: recorded walkthroughs and a safe practice copy of the real docs to learn on.

Result

A repeatable process the tech writing team used for its next 15+ new hires, with one place to learn everything.

04 FrameworksA consent framework for user data 3 tiers, 1 rule +

Problem

Growth teams wanted to use product data in new ways, and privacy rules differed by country.

Approach

Three tiers that match each use of customer data to the consent it needs, country by country, with clear human and platform responsibilities.

Result

If it stays in the product and serves the customer, it's covered. If it leaves for commercial use, consent comes first.

05 Curriculum · USCCritical media literacy, grades 7–8 Social media, search & AI +

Problem

Students meet social media, search, and AI every day, often without the tools to question what they see.

Approach

A curriculum unit grounded in learning and motivation research, with adaptations for different classrooms.

Result

A complete unit, ready to pilot with real students.
