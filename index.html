How I work

Sometimes it's AI. Sometimes it's a simple rule.

Every engagement follows the same three steps, with measurement built in from the first week.

(yes, I will tell you when you don't need AI)

  1. 1

    Strategize

    Find the real problem and where to go next, with data, interviews, and time where the work happens.

    set the baseline
  2. 2

    Build

    Design and build the solution with your people, testing it with them as we go.

    check in along the way
  3. 3

    Sustain

    Hand it off with clear ownership and room to grow, so it keeps working after I leave.

    show the results

Strategist · Problem solver · Designer · Thinker · Silly Goose

I've always preferred the spaces between the boxes.

I work in between organizations and the people they serve, engineers and designers, educators and companies, big ideas and the people who have to live in them.

Over ten years at a global software company, I grew from technical writer to owning the systems customers learned from: the help experience, AI help search, and the localization platform. I became the go-to consultant for 25+ teams on tools, workflows, and strategy.

I'm a chaos wrangler, partly because I'm a little chaotic myself. I love a messy, shifting problem: finding its shape, choosing the right tool or the right rule, and getting everyone moving in the same direction.

Now I'm earning an M.Ed. in Learning Design with AI and Emerging Technologies at USC. Off the clock, I'm a writer, musician, and dancer in San Jose.
